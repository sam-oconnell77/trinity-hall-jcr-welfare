Activities and Events

Wellbeing activities, socials and events in Halls. Check Instagram for what is on right now.

🎉 What welfare runs

Across the year the welfare team puts on wellbeing activities, socials and events around Halls, from chilled evenings and movement sessions to exam-time de-stress and themed welfare weeks. The line-up changes every term, so nothing here is fixed.

📣 How to find out what's on

Everything is announced on our Instagram first, and posters go up around Halls and in Oldham House. If you are not sure whether something is running, message us on Instagram or ask any team member.

💡 Got an idea?

If there is something you would like to see happen in Halls, tell us. Ideas from residents are how most of our events start.
